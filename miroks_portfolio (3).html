// Workflow

How I Build

A clean, structured pipeline from brief to final deliverable.

01 —
Concept & Block-out
Reference gathering, silhouette blocking, and proportion studies before committing to detail work.
02 —
High-Poly Sculpt
Detail passes, boolean operations, surface bevels — building the source mesh for baking.
03 —
Retopo & UVs
Clean quad-based retopology, optimized UV islands, and normal bake validation.
04 —
Texture & Render
Substance Painter texturing, final lighting setup, and high-quality presentation renders.
